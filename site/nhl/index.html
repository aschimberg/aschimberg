Record

Picks lock at the last line before kickoff and are graded straight up and against the spread. Pushes count as neither.

CLV (closing line value) compares the number when the model first made a pick with the closing number. Positive means the market moved toward our side after we picked it. It's the standard early read on whether picks have an edge, and it becomes meaningful within a few hundred picks, well before win–loss records do. Hover a pick's CLV for both numbers. Backtest picks have no CLV, since the backtest only had closing lines.
